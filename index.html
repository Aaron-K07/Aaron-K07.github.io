Portable Metrology Jig

The problem
Checking a structural frame meant scanning it and transporting it for inspection, a cycle that took about three weeks per frame.
What I built
A portable fixture that brings the measurement to the frame. It scans for surface defects and measures feature-to-feature distances across the whole frame right where it sits on the shop floor.
My role
I owned it end to end: concept CAD, prototyping, and on-site validation.
Result
Inspection now happens in place in under one minute instead of three weeks. The same summer on Canam's Operational Excellence team, I also led a $22.8K consumables cost reduction.

Proj-004 · Details1st place, NSS 2025

ASTRAEUS II: Eden Garden Module

The brief
ASTRAEUS II is a self-sustaining settlement for about 313,000 people on a journey to the exoplanet Trappist-1e. Eden Garden is its agriculture module, designed by our 12-person team.
Module design
A bioreactor core turns organic waste into nutrients, ringed by aeroponic, hydroponic, and soil-based growing zones on one closed-loop water network. Modules stack 16 high into 24 m × 54.4 m sectors of 1.66 acres each; 262 of them give the settlement 435 acres of farmland.
Bladeless tilling
We made a magnetorheological fluid by synthesizing iron nanoparticles from green tea extract and ferric chloride, then suspending them in oil. Under a magnet it stiffens into spikes, so flexible containers can till soil with no blades or heavy machinery.
Other systems
An autonomous pollination vehicle with LIDAR, cameras and an onboard AI processor, plus LED schedules tuned by growth stage: blue light for vegetative growth, red for flowering and fruiting, 14 hours a day.
My role
Led structural design and systems integration of the module.

ElectrolysisPrototypingClean energyPropulsion
Proj-005 · DetailsComplete

HHO Generator & Ion Thruster

HHO generator
An electrolysis cell that splits water into hydrogen and oxygen using an alkaline electrolyte (KOH or NaOH) to raise conductivity. I explored what drives its efficiency: electrolyte concentration, electrode surface area, operating temperature, and pulsed versus steady DC.
Ion thruster
A small-scale thruster using air as propellant: a copper-tube ionization chamber with a steel-brush cathode. A high-voltage DC supply ionizes the air and the electric field accelerates the ions to produce thrust, visible as a blue glow.
Why it matters
Both builds fed directly into the propulsion and fuelling sections of the award-winning ASTRAEUS II settlement design.
