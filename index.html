Project

Portable Metrology Jig

Canam Group internship, 2026 · Deployed, passed Gage R&R

Problem
Checking whether the holes on a structural part lined up meant scanning the part and transporting it for inspection, a cycle of about three weeks.
Fixture
A 3D-printed fixture holds the camera in a fixed position over the part, so a single calibration converts pixels to millimetres for every photo taken on the shop floor.
Software
A Python tool with an OpenCV and Matplotlib interface. It zooms to each hole, the inspector clicks near the top edge, and OpenCV snaps to the true peak using local Otsu thresholding, blurring and morphological cleanup to ignore shadows. The vertical offset between the two holes is converted to millimetres against a calibration block and judged against a 3 mm tolerance.
Traceability
Every sample is saved as an annotated image with close-up crops, filed by result, and logged to a CSV with a timestamp. A manual override exists, but it asks for confirmation, and every reading taken with it is flagged in the log.
Result
The system passed a Gage R&R study, and an inspection now takes under one minute in place instead of three weeks. I owned it end to end: concept CAD, the printed fixture, the code and on-site validation.

ASTRAEUS II: Eden Garden Module

NSS Live in a Healthy Space Design Competition, 2025 · 1st place

Brief
ASTRAEUS II is a self-sustaining settlement for about 313,000 people on a journey to the exoplanet Trappist-1e. Eden Garden is its agriculture module, designed by our 12-person team.
Module design
A bioreactor core turns organic waste into nutrients, ringed by aeroponic, hydroponic and soil-based growing zones on one closed-loop water network. Modules stack 16 high into 24 m × 54.4 m sectors of 1.66 acres each; 262 of them give the settlement 435 acres of farmland.
Bladeless tilling
We made a magnetorheological fluid by synthesizing iron nanoparticles from green tea extract and ferric chloride, then suspending them in oil. Under a magnet it stiffens into spikes, so flexible containers can till soil with no blades or heavy machinery.
Other systems
An autonomous pollination vehicle with LIDAR, cameras and an onboard AI processor, plus LED schedules tuned by growth stage: blue light for vegetative growth, red for flowering and fruiting, 14 hours a day.
My role
I led structural design and systems integration of the module.

HHO Generator & Ion Thruster

Independent, 2024

HHO generator
An electrolysis cell that splits water into hydrogen and oxygen, using an alkaline electrolyte (KOH or NaOH) to raise conductivity. I explored what drives its efficiency: electrolyte concentration, electrode surface area, operating temperature, and pulsed versus steady DC.
Ion thruster
A small-scale thruster that uses air as propellant: a copper-tube ionization chamber with a steel-brush cathode. A high-voltage DC supply ionizes the air, and the electric field accelerates the ions to produce thrust, visible as a blue glow.
Where it led
Both builds fed into the propulsion and fuelling sections of the award-winning ASTRAEUS II settlement design.
